Who is MERIT for?+

Founders building an audience on LinkedIn, creators with a business audience, and startup GTM or demand-generation teams who want to turn engagement into a more organized prospecting workflow.

What does MERIT actually find?+

People engaging with selected LinkedIn profiles, company pages, competitor content, and relevant conversations. MERIT is designed to help you prioritize those people against your ideal customer profile and retain the context behind each signal.

Does engagement mean someone is ready to buy?+

No, and we won't pretend it does. Engagement gives you a useful reason to investigate and start a more relevant conversation. ICP fit and the original context help your team decide whether to follow up.
